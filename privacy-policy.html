4. BIOMETRIC INFORMATION POLICY

This policy specifically governs our collection, retention, and use of your Biometric Information, which is categorized as Sensitive Personal Information under applicable state laws.

A. Collection and Purpose We only collect your Biometric Information with your explicit and voluntary consent. The types of Biometric Information collected, and their limited purposes, are:

  • Voiceprint Templates (for ZapVoice): Used solely to generate synthesized audio for text messages and for account authentication/verification.

Zapshot does not use, share, or sell your Biometric Information for advertising, profiling, or to infer characteristics about you.

B. Disclosure and Processing Partners We do not Sell or share your Biometric Information. We share this information only with trusted third-party Service Providers solely for the business purpose of enabling the features you have requested. These partners include:

  • ElevenLabs: Assists with the creation and use of voice models for audio synthesis.
  • Cloudinary: Hosts user-generated content, which may contain voice recordings used in the creation of voice models.

We require all partners processing this sensitive data to be contractually bound to maintain confidentiality and use the information only for specified purposes.

C. Retention and Destruction We will retain your Biometric Information only as long as is necessary to fulfill the initial, stated purpose of collection. We will permanently destroy your Biometric Information from our systems, and direct our service providers to do the same, upon the earliest of the following events:

  1. The initial purpose for collecting the Biometric Data has been satisfied.
  2. Your user account is deleted.
  3. Your account remains inactive for thirty (30) consecutive days (applies only to the volatile Voiceprint Templates for ZapVoice functionality, as specified in our FAQ).
  4. Three (3) years from the date of the termination of your relationship with Zapshot.

We employ strict security safeguards, including implementing measures that meet or exceed the requirements of applicable state laws (e.g., BIPA and CPRA), to protect your Biometric Data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or use. We will not re-enroll your Voiceprint Templates without obtaining new, explicit consent from you.
